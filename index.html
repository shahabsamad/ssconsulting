About SS Consulting

Structural Engineering & Geospatial Experts

SS Consulting brings together a Design & Build division and a GIS Consultancy division under one roof, so every scheme is designed against real, surveyed ground conditions rather than assumptions.

Our team works across residential and commercial projects for developers, landowners and local authorities, specialising in structural design, drainage and SuDS, planning support, and site supervision through to handover.

  • Qualified engineers, surveyors and project managers in-house
  • QGIS and Google Earth Engine as standard consultancy tools
  • UK-wide site coverage with remote monitoring for any location

Design & Build

A Range of Consulting Services

Four disciplines, one consultancy — from feasibility through to a structure that's actually standing.

01

Feasibility & Site Assessment

Ground investigation, topographic survey, constraints mapping and outline appraisal, tested against the site before design begins.

02

Structural & Civil Design

Foundations, drainage, earthworks and structural detailing, developed to Building Regulations and relevant British Standards.

03

Planning & Approvals

Technical submissions for planning and building control, coordinated with local authorities and statutory consultees.

04

Construction & Delivery

Site supervision, contractor coordination and as-built verification through to handover, keeping the works true to design.
